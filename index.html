How to Play ❓

1. Move — ←→ / AD or drag on the arena. Catch falling capsules with the paddle.
2. Launch — Space / click / tap ❄️. Angle depends on where the ball hits the paddle.
3. Combo — break blocks without touching the paddle to raise the multiplier up to ×5.
4. Power-ups — ❄️ multi-ball · 🧊 wide · ⚡ dash · 💎 bonus · 🛡️ shield absorbs one fall.
5. Survive — 3 lives. Bedrock is indestructible. Clear all breakable blocks to advance.
